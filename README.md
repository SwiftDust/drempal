## Drempal: dodge the asteroids!

![image-1.png]

Thanks for stopping by! This is my first full game coded by only me in Godot, it's been
a blast!

The idea is simple: you're a slime who has to dodge the asteroids with his shield to survive.
In the meanwhile, don't get killed by the slimy drab that is coming higher and higher up!

The controls are as follows:

- WASD / arrow keys: move
- E: equip/disequip shield
- Mouse: tilt the shield
